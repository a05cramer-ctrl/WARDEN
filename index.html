Docs

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The extension is free and has no paid tier.

Does it need my wallet?

No. It only reads public posts and public chain data.

What do the colors mean?

Red is a scam or a rug. Amber is be careful. Green is a clean record. A grey line means there is not enough data yet.

Can it be wrong?

Yes. A green line is not a promise. It means nothing was flagged. Nothing here is financial advice.

Which browsers?

Chrome, Brave, Edge and Arc.
